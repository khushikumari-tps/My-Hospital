Appointments & Visits

How do I book an appointment?

Use the Book Appointment button in the header, call +91 9211221551, or write to info@advityahealthcares.com. Our coordinator confirms the slot and tells you what to bring.

What should I bring to my first consultation?
  • Photo ID and insurance card, if you have one
  • All previous prescriptions and discharge summaries
  • Recent scans — CT, MRI or ultrasound — including the films or CD, not just the report
  • Blood reports from the last three months
Do you offer teleconsultation for outstation patients?

Yes. If you are travelling from another city, a video review of your reports before you make the trip is usually the first step. Ask the coordinator when you call.

Pancreatic & GI Care

What conditions does the pancreaCare+ programme cover?

Acute and chronic pancreatitis, pancreatic cysts and tumours, pancreatic cancer, bile duct and gallbladder disease, and the surgical and nutritional care that goes with them. Read the story of pancreaCare+ for the full picture.

What are the warning signs of a pancreatic problem?

Persistent upper abdominal pain that radiates to the back, unexplained weight loss, jaundice, greasy or floating stools, and new-onset diabetes after 50. Any of these lasting more than a couple of weeks deserves a specialist review.

This page is general information, not a diagnosis. See a doctor about your own symptoms.

How long is recovery after major pancreatic surgery?

Most patients stay in hospital for 7–14 days depending on the procedure and their condition on admission. Returning to routine activity typically takes 6–8 weeks, with diet and enzyme support guided by our nutrition team throughout.

Admissions, Billing & Insurance

Do you accept health insurance and cashless claims?

We work with most major insurers and TPAs. Share your policy details at least 48 hours before a planned admission so pre-authorisation can be cleared in time. For emergencies, the desk starts the claim while treatment begins.

Can I get a cost estimate before admission?

Yes. Once the treating consultant confirms the plan, the billing desk issues a written estimate covering surgery, stay, consumables and expected investigations.

Do you help outstation families with stay and travel?

Our patient care coordinators help with nearby accommodation, appointment clustering so fewer trips are needed, and follow-up by phone once you are home.

Emergencies

What do I do in an emergency?

Call +91 9211221551 immediately, or dial the national emergency number. Do not wait for an appointment — severe abdominal pain with vomiting, fever or jaundice needs to be seen the same day.
