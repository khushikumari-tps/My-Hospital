What this website is for

This website describes the surgical and gastrointestinal services offered by Advitya Healthcares and explains the conditions those services treat, so that you can decide whether to arrange a consultation. It is an information and enquiry service.

Medical disclaimer

Medical disclaimer. The information provided on this page is for educational purposes only and should not be considered a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Treatment options may vary depending on individual clinical evaluation.

Reading this website does not create a doctor–patient relationship, and nothing on it is a diagnosis or a recommendation for your particular case. Whether an operation is appropriate, which approach suits you and what the likely course of recovery is can only be decided after a consultation and the relevant investigations. If your symptoms are severe or worsening, seek medical attention rather than waiting for an appointment.

Enquiries and appointments

Submitting the appointment form, calling or messaging on WhatsApp is a request for a consultation. An appointment exists once the team has confirmed a date, time and venue with you. Please do not send detailed clinical information through the website form.

Accuracy

The medical information here is written to be accurate and is kept under review by the clinical team. Medical practice changes, and pages may not always reflect the most recent guidance at the moment you read them. If something here matters to a decision you are making, ask about it at your consultation.
