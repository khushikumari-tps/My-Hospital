Privacy policy

This page explains what happens to the details you send through this website, and what the site measures while you are reading it.

What we collect

The appointment enquiry form asks for your name, phone number, a preferred date and time, the surgery or condition you are enquiring about, and an optional message. Nothing else is required, and the form asks you not to include detailed medical information.

If you call or message us on WhatsApp instead, we hold whatever you choose to tell us in that conversation.

How your enquiry reaches us

When you submit the form, the details are placed into an email addressed to info@advityahealthcares.com and sent from your own email account. The website does not store your enquiry itself. The team uses the details to contact you about your consultation, and for nothing else.

Medical records

Reports, scans and clinical records you share before or during a consultation are handled as part of your care by the treating team and the hospital where you are seen, not through this website. Please do not send clinical documents through the enquiry form.

Measurement and advertising

These pages are used for Google Ads campaigns. To know which advertisements lead to enquiries, the site uses Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads conversion tracking. Those tools record actions such as a page view, a click on a call, WhatsApp or appointment button, and a form submission. They do not receive what you type into the form.

Google sets cookies or similar identifiers to do this. You can block or clear them in your browser settings; the pages work either way. Google's own handling of that data is described in its privacy policy.

Sharing

Your enquiry is seen by the Advitya Healthcares team and, where your care requires it, the hospital or alliance partner at which you will be seen. It is not sold, and it is not shared for anyone else's marketing.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about your enquiry, ask for it to be corrected, or ask us to stop contacting you. Write to info@advityahealthcares.com or call +91 92112 21551 and say what you would like done.

Security

This website is served over HTTPS, so what passes between your browser and the site is encrypted in transit. No system is perfect, which is another reason the form asks for as little as it does.

Changes

If the way enquiries are handled changes — for example if a booking system is added — this page will be updated to say so.
