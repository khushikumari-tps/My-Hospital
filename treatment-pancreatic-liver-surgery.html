Pancreatic and Liver Surgery

Evaluation and surgery for conditions of the pancreas and liver at Advitya Hospital Baruipur.

Department: GI, Liver and Pancreatic Surgery

Call +91 9211221552
Is This You?

Symptoms and Conditions Treated

Conditions treated

  • Tumours and cysts of the pancreas
  • Tumours of the liver
  • Complications of pancreatitis
  • Blockage of the bile duct

Common symptoms

  • Yellowing of the eyes or skin (jaundice)
  • Pain in the upper abdomen that may spread to the back
  • Unexplained weight loss or loss of appetite
  • Dark urine or pale stools
  • A growth or cyst seen on a scan
Don’t Wait

When Should You Consult a Doctor?

See a doctor if

  • You develop jaundice
  • A scan has shown a growth or cyst in the pancreas or liver
  • You have ongoing upper abdominal pain or weight loss

Get emergency help at once if you have

  • Jaundice with fever or shivering
  • Severe upper abdominal pain

What Can Be Done

Available Treatment Options

Evaluation

Blood tests and scans help your doctor understand the problem and plan treatment.

Pancreatic surgery

Surgery to remove the affected part of the pancreas, such as the Whipple procedure, when it is suitable.

Liver surgery

Surgery to remove the affected part of the liver, when it is suitable.

Other treatment

Some conditions are treated without surgery, or with other treatment alongside surgery. Your doctor will advise.

The right treatment is decided only after your doctor has examined you and reviewed your reports.

Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is pancreatic or liver surgery a major operation?

Yes. These are major operations, and your doctor will explain the benefits and risks in detail before any decision is made.

Can I send my reports before visiting?

Yes. Sharing your scan and blood reports in advance helps the doctor guide you. Call or WhatsApp the hospital.

Will I need other treatment after surgery?

Some patients do. This depends on the condition and is decided by the treating team.

Does every growth in the pancreas mean cancer?

No. Some growths and cysts are not cancer. Tests help find out what it is.

This page gives general information and is not a substitute for medical advice. Please consult a doctor about your own condition.
