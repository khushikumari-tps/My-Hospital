About PancreaCare+

PancreaCare+ was founded with a steadfast commitment to creating a world-class centre of excellence dedicated entirely to pancreatic health. The "Pancrea" in our name reflects our unwavering focus on pancreatic diseases, while "Care" embodies the compassionate values that drive us. The "+" symbolises our ambition to grow and create a meaningful impact within the specialised field of pancreatic healthcare.

It is our humble initiative to address Pancreatic Health and cure aggressively. We continuously strive to upgrade our skills, adopt the latest techniques, and offer state-of-the-art, cost-effective treatments, providing hope and healing to patients across all communities.

Clinical Focus

With specialised expertise in pancreatic, biliary, and liver surgery, I focus on:

  • Complex pancreatic cancer surgery, including Whipple procedures and vascular resections
  • Minimally invasive and robotic surgery for pancreatic and gastrointestinal diseases
  • Management of acute and chronic pancreatitis
  • Hepato-biliary oncology and advanced GI cancer treatments
  • Postoperative outcomes and patient rehabilitation
The Vision Behind PancreaCare+

PancreaCare+ was established to connect specialised surgical expertise with accessible patient education. Too often, patients encounter misinformation, delays in diagnosis, and obstacles to obtaining expert care. Through PancreaCare+, I aim to offer accurate medical insights, evidence-based treatment strategies, and educational resources to empower patients and their families.

Research & Academic Contributions

I am actively engaged in clinical research, particularly focusing on predictive analytics for postoperative outcomes and the role of vascular resections in pancreatic surgery. My work also addresses gender-related challenges in surgical specialities and includes mentoring future surgeons. I serve as a reviewer for prominent medical journals and contribute to national and international surgical registries.

Services Offered

Pancreatic Surgery

  • Whipple procedure (Pancreaticoduodenectomy)
  • Distal and total pancreatectomy
  • Minimally invasive and robotic-assisted pancreatic surgery
  • Surgical management of pancreatic cysts and neuroendocrine tumours
  • Vascular resections in pancreatic cancer surgery
  • Surgical drainage of pancreatic pseudocysts
  • Necrosectomy for infected pancreatic necrosis
  • Surgery for chronic pancreatitis

Biliary and Liver Surgery

  • Hepatectomy for liver tumors and metastases
  • Bile duct resections and reconstructions
  • Surgery for bile duct injuries and strictures
  • Management of gallbladder cancer
  • Liver cyst and abscess drainage procedures

Gastrointestinal Oncology

  • Surgery for stomach and colorectal cancers
  • HIPEC (Hyperthermic Intraperitoneal Chemotherapy) for advanced GI cancers
  • Laparoscopic and robotic-assisted gastrointestinal surgeries

Benign GI Conditions

  • Surgery for gastrointestinal stromal tumors (GISTs)
  • Fundoplication for GERD and hiatal hernias
  • Surgery for small bowel obstructions and diverticular disease
  • Management of achalasia and esophageal motility disorders

Advanced Minimal Access Surgery

  • Laparoscopic and robotic gastrointestinal surgeries
  • Keyhole surgery for hernia repairs (inguinal, incisional, and diaphragmatic hernias)
