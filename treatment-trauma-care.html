Trauma Care

Emergency care for injuries at Advitya Hospital Baruipur, with ICU and critical care support.

Department: Trauma Surgery and Critical Care

Call +91 9211221552 Emergency: +91 9211221553
Is This You?

Symptoms and Conditions Treated

Conditions treated

  • Injuries from road accidents
  • Falls and other accidents
  • Injuries to the abdomen or chest
  • Deep cuts and wounds
  • Injured patients who need intensive care

Common symptoms

  • Heavy bleeding
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Loss of consciousness, confusion or vomiting after a head injury
  • Severe pain in the abdomen or chest after an injury
  • Being unable to move a limb
Don’t Wait

When Should You Consult a Doctor?

See a doctor if

  • Any significant injury should be checked by a doctor
  • Pain or swelling after a fall or accident does not improve

Get emergency help at once if you have

  • Heavy bleeding
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Unconsciousness or confusion after an injury

Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do before reaching the hospital?

Call the emergency number, keep the injured person still and press firmly on any bleeding with a clean cloth.

Should I call before coming?

Yes, if you can. Calling helps the team prepare. Do not delay travel to make the call.

What documents should I bring?

A photo ID of the patient, such as an Aadhaar card or Voter ID card. Never delay emergency care for documents.

Is an ambulance service available?

Yes. Call the hospital for ambulance services.

This page gives general information and is not a substitute for medical advice. Please consult a doctor about your own condition.
