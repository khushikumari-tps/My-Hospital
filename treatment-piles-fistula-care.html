Piles and Fistula Care

Assessment and treatment of piles, fissures and fistula at Advitya Hospital Baruipur.

Department: Surgical Gastroenterology · General and Laparoscopic Surgery

Is This You?

Symptoms and Conditions Treated

Conditions treated

  • Piles (haemorrhoids)
  • Anal fissure
  • Fistula-in-ano
  • Perianal abscess

Common symptoms

  • Bleeding during or after passing stool
  • Pain or burning around the anus
  • Swelling or a lump near the anus
  • Itching or discomfort
  • Pus or discharge near the anus
Don’t Wait

When Should You Consult a Doctor?

See a doctor if

  • You notice blood in your stool
  • Pain, swelling or discharge around the anus does not improve
  • You have a change in bowel habits along with bleeding

Get emergency help at once if you have

  • Heavy bleeding from the back passage
  • Severe pain and swelling with fever

What Can Be Done

Available Treatment Options

Diet and medicines

Early piles and fissures often improve with a high-fibre diet, enough fluids and medicines prescribed by your doctor.

Procedures for piles

Some piles are treated with minor procedures. Your doctor will advise whether this is suitable.

Surgery

Surgery may be advised for advanced piles, for a fistula, or to drain an abscess.

The right treatment is decided only after your doctor has examined you and reviewed your reports.

Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is rectal bleeding always caused by piles?

No. Bleeding can have other causes, so it should always be checked by a doctor.

Can piles come back after treatment?

They can. A high-fibre diet, enough water and avoiding straining help reduce the risk.

Does a fistula heal with medicines?

A fistula usually needs surgery. Your surgeon will explain the options.

I feel shy talking about this. What should I do?

These are common conditions that doctors treat every day. Sharing your symptoms openly helps you get the right care.
