Our Social Service

Free screening camps, outreach and health awareness, at no cost to those who attend.

Advitya Healthcares runs free screening camps and outreach programmes in places where a specialist opinion normally means a long journey and a day's lost wages. The work is carried out by the same surgical team that operates at the hospital.

What we do Partner with us
  • Ghaghra, Jharkhand
  • Free for everyone who attends
  • Run by the hospital's own surgical team
What we do

The work, as it stands today

Four strands, all run without charge to the people who attend.

Free GI screening camps

Camps in rural Jharkhand, including Ghaghra, where villagers are examined for digestive and liver complaints by our surgical team, at no cost.

Community outreach

Visits to neighbourhoods and partner organisations, taking consultations and basic checks to people who would otherwise travel a long way for them.

Health awareness

Sessions on digestive health and the early signs of GI cancer, so a symptom is checked rather than waited out.

Referral support

Anyone found at a camp to need surgery is guided to the right department, so a diagnosis in a village does not end there.

The day itself

How a camp runs

From the first phone call to the follow-up, this is what a camp day looks like.

  1. 1

    A host invites us

    A panchayat, school, employer or voluntary body asks for a camp and provides a hall or a block office for the day.

  2. 2

    The team travels

    Surgeons and staff travel with registration desks, basic equipment and medicines.

  3. 3

    People are seen

    Each person is registered, examined and counselled about what the symptom means and what to do next.

  4. 4

    Care continues

    Those who need tests or surgery are guided to the hospital, so the visit leads somewhere.

Affordable, ethical care, so that no family feels forced to leave their city for the proper treatment.

Dr. Deeksha Kapoor · CEO & Medical Head, Advitya Healthcares
