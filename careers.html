Why Build Your Career With Us

Advitya exists to bring specialist surgical and pancreatic care to the places that have gone without it. That mission needs clinicians and support teams who want their work to reach further than a single operating room.

Work Alongside Specialists

Learn directly from consultants in HPB surgery, surgical oncology and interventional gastroenterology on complex, high-volume cases.

Reach Beyond Metro Cities

Our programmes are designed for Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities, so the care you deliver reaches patients who would otherwise travel for days.

Structured Learning

Case reviews, skill workshops and protocol-led practice are part of the routine, not an afterthought.
