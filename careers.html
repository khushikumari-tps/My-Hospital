Careers That Carry Care Further

Advitya brings advanced Gastrointestinal and Hepato-Pancreato-Biliary care closer to the people who need it. That work is built by clinicians, technicians and support teams who want their day to reach past one hospital floor.

Why Advitya

Specialist work, with room to grow into it

Our units run GI, HPB and surgical oncology programmes end to end — clinic, theatre, recovery and follow-up — so the people who join us see the whole pathway, not one slice of it.

Work alongside specialists

Consultants in HPB surgery, surgical oncology and interventional gastroenterology, on complex cases you rarely meet in a general unit.

Reach beyond the metros

Kolkata HQ with partner hospitals across West Bengal, Jharkhand, Bihar and Odisha — care that travels to the patient instead of the other way round.

Training that is scheduled

Skill workshops, protocol-led practice and structured specialisation — from perioperative nursing to stoma care — are part of the roster.

Room to move

An expanding platform means new units, new theatres and internal growth paths rather than a fixed ceiling.
