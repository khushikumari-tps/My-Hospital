Hernia Surgery

Assessment and surgical repair of hernias at Advitya Hospital Baruipur.

Department: General and Laparoscopic Surgery

Book an Appointment Call +91 9211221552
Is This You?

Symptoms and Conditions Treated

Conditions treated

  • Inguinal hernia (in the groin)
  • Umbilical hernia (at or near the navel)
  • Incisional hernia (at the site of an old operation scar)
  • Other abdominal wall hernias

Common symptoms

  • A bulge or swelling in the groin or abdomen
  • A bulge that may go back in when you lie down
  • Discomfort or pain when lifting, coughing or straining
  • A feeling of heaviness or dragging in the groin
Don’t Wait

When Should You Consult a Doctor?

See a doctor if

  • You notice a new bulge or swelling
  • The bulge is getting bigger or more uncomfortable
  • A hernia is affecting your work or daily activities

Get emergency help at once if you have

  • The bulge becomes painful, hard or cannot be pushed back
  • Vomiting, or being unable to pass stool or gas, along with a hernia

What Can Be Done

Available Treatment Options

Watchful waiting

A small hernia that causes no symptoms may be monitored, if your doctor advises it.

Open hernia repair

The hernia is repaired through a cut over the swelling, usually with a mesh to strengthen the area.

Laparoscopic (keyhole) hernia repair

The repair is done through small cuts using a camera, where this approach is suitable.

The right treatment is decided only after your doctor has examined you and reviewed your reports.

Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a hernia go away on its own?

No. A hernia does not heal by itself. Some small hernias can be watched, but many need repair.

Is a mesh used in hernia repair?

A mesh is commonly used to strengthen the repair. Your surgeon will explain whether it is right for you.

When can I return to work?

It depends on the type of repair and the work you do. Your surgeon will advise you.

Can a hernia come back after surgery?

It can happen in some cases. Following your surgeon's advice after the operation helps lower the risk.

This page gives general information and is not a substitute for medical advice. Please consult a doctor about your own condition.
