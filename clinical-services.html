Clinical Services

Digestive, pancreatic and surgical care under one roof

Looking for a department rather than a service?

The services below are where Advitya goes deepest — the digestive system. Advitya Hospital Baruipur’s confirmed specialities also include general and laparoscopic surgery, urology and andrology, and trauma surgery and critical care.

See all departments

Gastroenterology

Assessment and treatment of the full digestive tract — from reflux and ulcers to inflammatory bowel disease and chronic abdominal pain that has gone undiagnosed elsewhere.

  • Acid reflux, gastritis and peptic ulcer disease
  • Irritable bowel syndrome and inflammatory bowel disease
  • Chronic diarrhoea, constipation and malabsorption
  • GI bleeding evaluation

Pancreatic Disorders

Our PancreaCare+ programme is built around one of the hardest organs in the abdomen to treat well. Read how it started, or what a healthy pancreas does.

Pancreatitis

Acute and chronic pancreatitis managed end to end — pain control, nutrition, drainage of collections, enzyme replacement, and surgery when the disease demands it.

Pancreatic Cancer

Staging, surgery where possible including Whipple's procedure, and follow-up. Early referral changes what is possible.

Surgical Care

Cysts, pseudocysts, ductal disease and complex reoperations, handled by a team that does this work regularly rather than occasionally.

Liver Care

Evaluation and management of liver and biliary disease, with further tests arranged as needed.

  • Fatty liver disease and cirrhosis
  • Hepatitis evaluation and monitoring
  • Jaundice workup and bile duct obstruction
  • Gallbladder and gallstone disease

Endoscopy

Diagnostic and therapeutic upper GI endoscopy performed as a day procedure in most cases.

  • Upper GI endoscopy and biopsy
  • ERCP for bile and pancreatic duct disease
  • Stenting, dilatation and bleeding control

GI Surgery

Open and laparoscopic surgery across the gastrointestinal tract, including cancer resections with oncology input from the outset.

  • Hepatobiliary and pancreatic (HPB) surgery
  • Colorectal and gastric cancer surgery
  • Laparoscopic gallbladder, hernia and appendix surgery
  • Redo and complex abdominal surgery

Colonoscopy

Screening and diagnostic colonoscopy with polyp removal in the same sitting where indicated.

  • Screening from age 45, or earlier with family history
  • Investigation of bleeding, anaemia or altered bowel habit
  • Polypectomy and biopsy
  • Surveillance after previous polyps or cancer

Nutrition & Dietetics

Digestive and pancreatic disease changes what the body can absorb. Nutrition advice is part of treatment and recovery.

  • Pre-operative nutritional optimisation
  • Pancreatic enzyme replacement guidance
  • Post-surgical diet progression
  • Diabetes and weight management support

Emergency Care

Severe abdominal pain with vomiting, fever or jaundice needs to be seen the same day. Call +91 9211221553 — do not wait for a scheduled appointment.

  • Acute abdomen and suspected pancreatitis
  • GI bleeding
  • Obstructive jaundice and cholangitis
  • Post-operative complications
