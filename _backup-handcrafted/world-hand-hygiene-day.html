Hand washing is the foundation of infection control. On World Hand Hygiene Day, a note on why it matters more inside a hospital than anywhere else.

Advitya Healthcares 5 May 2026

Why hand hygiene matters in hospitals

Patients in hospital are already vulnerable. Acquiring an infection during treatment adds a risk they did not arrive with.

  • Clean hands prevent infection passing from person to person
  • They protect patients through treatment and recovery
  • They keep the hospital environment genuinely safe

How we maintain hygiene at Advitya Healthcares

  • Regular hand sanitisation by all clinical staff
  • Disinfected patient areas
  • Documented infection control practice
  • Ongoing maintenance of a safe environment

Simple habits you can follow

  • Wash properly with soap — time it, do not rush it
  • Use sanitiser when you are out
  • Clean your hands before eating
  • Avoid touching your face repeatedly

A message for our community

Clean, safe healthcare is not a campaign that runs for one day a year. It is a daily commitment to the people who walk through the door.
