What We Do

We are dedicated to providing end-to-end care for pancreatic, biliary, liver, and gastrointestinal (GI) diseases. From initial diagnosis to long-term management and recovery, we deliver comprehensive, patient-centered services that address every aspect of your health journey. Whether managing a benign condition or navigating a complex malignancy, we are here to provide you with the best possible care with expertise and compassion.

Minimally invasive gastrointestinal surgery in India

01

Advanced Diagnostics

Accurate and timely diagnosis is the cornerstone of effective treatment. We offer:

  • High-resolution imaging, including MRI, CT, PET scans, and advanced ultrasound technologies.
  • Endoscopic procedures include ERCP (Endoscopic Retrograde Cholangiopancreatography) and EUS (Endoscopic Ultrasound) for minimally invasive evaluations.
  • Cutting-edge genetic testing and biomarker analysis to tailor personalized care plans.
02

Specialized Surgical and Interventional Care

Our team specializes in complex and innovative surgical solutions, including:

  • Pancreatic Surgeries: Whipple procedures, minimally invasive pancreatectomies, and surgeries for chronic pancreatitis, cysts, and neuroendocrine tumors.
  • Liver Surgeries: Resections for liver cancers and benign tumors, minimally invasive liver surgeries, and bile duct reconstructions.
  • Biliary Surgeries: Gallbladder cancer surgeries, bile duct reconstructions, and removal of complex bile duct stones.
  • Gastrointestinal Surgeries: Advanced procedures for colorectal, gastric, and esophageal cancers.
  • Vascular Resections: Reconstruction of critical blood vessels impacted by advanced tumors.
03

Comprehensive Medical Management

We provide holistic non-surgical care for:

  • Acute and chronic pancreatitis.
  • Liver diseases such as cirrhosis, fatty liver disease, and hepatitis.
  • Gallbladder and bile duct disorders.
  • GI diseases including GERD, IBS, and IBD.
  • Nutritional management for malabsorption and related conditions.
04

Integrated Palliative and Supportive Care

For patients with advanced or inoperable conditions, we offer:

  • Symptom-focused care to enhance comfort and quality of life.
  • Emotional and psychological support for patients and their families.
  • Nutritional counselling to improve strength and well-being during treatment.
05

Preventive Care and Education

We believe in empowering our patients through education and proactive care. Our offerings include:

  • Regular screenings for early detection of pancreatic, liver, biliary, and GI cancers.
  • Lifestyle modification plans to reduce risk factors and prevent disease progression.
  • Comprehensive educational resources to help patients and caregivers make informed decisions.

Multidisciplinary care for digestive tract cancers

At PancreaCare+, our philosophy is built on delivering care that addresses every step of the patient journey:

  • Multidisciplinary Teamwork: Our team of surgeons, gastroenterologists, hepatologists, oncologists, dietitians, and rehabilitation experts works seamlessly to provide integrated care.

  • Advanced Technology: We utilise the latest surgical techniques, robotic-assisted procedures, and precision medicine to achieve optimal outcomes.

  • Personalised Care Plans: Every patient is unique, and we design customised treatment strategies to meet their individual needs and goals.

  • Continuity of Care: From diagnosis and treatment to recovery and follow-up, we provide continuous support to ensure long-term health and wellness.

Why patients trust our pancreatic cancer surgeons

  • Comprehensive Expertise: Our team is experienced in managing common and rare conditions of the pancreas, biliary system, liver, and GI tract.

  • Seamless Care Coordination: We manage all aspects of care under one roof, ensuring a smooth and stress-free experience for our patients.

  • Patient-Centred Philosophy: We prioritise your well-being, addressing not only physical health but also emotional and psychological needs.

  • Commitment to Innovation: By staying at the forefront of medical advancements, we deliver treatments that are effective, minimally invasive, and tailored to each patient.

Whether you’re seeking a diagnosis or treatment, PancreaCare+ is here to guide you through every stage of your healthcare journey. We aim to help you achieve better health, faster recovery, and a brighter future.
