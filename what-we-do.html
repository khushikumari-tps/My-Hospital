The Programme

What We Do

We are dedicated to providing end-to-end care for pancreatic, biliary, liver, and gastrointestinal (GI) diseases. From initial diagnosis to long-term management and recovery, we deliver comprehensive, patient-centered services that address every aspect of your health journey.

01

Advanced Diagnostics

  • High-resolution imaging (MRI, CT, PET scans, ultrasound)
  • Endoscopic procedures (ERCP, EUS)
  • Genetic testing and biomarker analysis
02

Specialized Surgical and Interventional Care

  • Pancreatic Surgeries (Whipple procedures, pancreatectomies)
  • Liver Surgeries (resections, minimally invasive approaches)
  • Biliary Surgeries (gallbladder cancer, bile duct work)
  • Gastrointestinal Surgeries (colorectal, gastric, esophageal)
  • Vascular Resections
03

Comprehensive Medical Management

  • Acute and chronic pancreatitis
  • Liver diseases (cirrhosis, fatty liver, hepatitis)
  • Gallbladder and bile duct disorders
  • GI diseases (GERD, IBS, IBD)
  • Nutritional management
04

Integrated Palliative and Supportive Care

  • Symptom-focused care
  • Emotional and psychological support
  • Nutritional counseling
05

Preventive Care and Education

  • Regular cancer screenings
  • Lifestyle modification plans
  • Educational resources
