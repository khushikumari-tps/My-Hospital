OPD Schedule

Advitya Hospital Baruipur, operated by Advitya Healthcares Pvt Ltd, offers OPD consultations with doctors in Surgical Gastroenterology, General Surgery and Orthopaedics. OPD days and timings are published on this page only after the hospital has confirmed them. Where a doctor’s schedule is not fixed, request a preferred date and the hospital will call you to confirm availability. To book a visit, call +91 9211221552 or book an appointment online.
