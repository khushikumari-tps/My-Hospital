Emergency and Admission Information

Emergency contact — 24×7

For a medical emergency, call Advitya Hospital Baruipur on either number.

+91 9211221553 +91 9211221552

Ambulance services are available — call the hospital.

Admission process

  1. Call the hospital on +91 9211221552 or +91 9211221553.
  2. Share the patient’s details — name, age and the problem. The hospital team will guide you on the next steps.
  3. Come to the hospital with the documents listed below.

Documents required during admission

  • A government photo ID of the patient, such as an Aadhaar card or a Voter ID card

Also helpful: any previous medical reports and prescriptions.

Discharge procedure

This depends on the patient’s condition and treatment. The treating team will explain the discharge process.

Visiting hours

Visiting hours depend on the patient’s condition. Please check with the hospital team.

Patient and attendant guidelines

These depend on the patient’s condition. The hospital team will explain what patients and attendants need to follow.

Paying For It

Insurance, Cashless and TPA

Accepted insurance companies

List will be published once confirmed

To check whether your insurer is accepted, call +91 9211221552.

TPA partners

List will be published once confirmed

To check whether your TPA is accepted, call +91 9211221552.

Documents required

  • Health insurance card or e-card
  • Policy number and policy details
  • Government photo ID of the patient, such as an Aadhaar card or Voter ID card
  • The doctor’s advice for admission and any relevant reports

Please confirm the documents with the hospital before admission.

Cashless approval process

  1. Tell the hospital at admission that you have health insurance.
  2. Pre-authorisation — a request is sent to your insurance company or TPA.
  3. Review — the insurance company or TPA reviews the request and replies.
  4. Settlement — if approved, the covered amount is settled directly. Charges that are not covered are paid by the patient.
Cashless treatment is possible only when your insurance company or TPA is accepted by the hospital. Accepted companies and TPA partners will be listed here once confirmed.
