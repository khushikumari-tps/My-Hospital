Welcome to PancreaCare+, where expertise meets compassion in treating pancreato-biliary and gastrointestinal diseases.

I am Dr Deeksha Kapoor, an accomplished pancreato-biliary and gastrointestinal surgeon with a strong foundation in advanced surgical oncology and minimally invasive procedures. With extensive training and experience from premier institutions in India and abroad, I am committed to providing world-class, patient-centered care.

If patients cannot access the finest surgical and cancer care, the finest care should be brought to them.

That belief is why Advitya does not begin with a building. It begins with the hospitals already serving second- and third-tier cities — and with the expertise, technology and training we bring to them, so families are not asked to leave home to be treated well.

Dr. Deeksha Kapoor CEO & Medical Head, Advitya Healthcares
